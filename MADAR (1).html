Rules & missions

Battery threshold

The minimum charge a satellite needs to accept or keep a mission. The Battery Agent watches every satellite; once one falls below this line it raises a LOW_BATTERY alert and the Coordinator moves its mission to another satellite. A higher threshold makes the system more cautious, so faults trigger sooner. (Satellites also have a built-in hard minimum of 30%.)

Number of missions

How many observation requests the Mission Agent receives (all target Riyadh). There are only two satellites, so asking for three or more creates a scheduling conflict: the extra missions wait in the queue until a satellite finishes.

Priorities click to cycle

The order in which the Coordinator hands out missions: High first, then Medium, then Low. When satellites are scarce, the low-priority missions are the ones left waiting.
